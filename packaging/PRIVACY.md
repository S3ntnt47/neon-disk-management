# Neon Disk Management Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Neon Disk Management is a partition manager that runs entirely on your own computer.

**What the app accesses.** The app reads the list of disks, partitions and volumes on your PC through the Windows Storage Management API: names, sizes, file systems, labels and free space. It changes a disk only when you choose an action and confirm it. Those actions are resizing, creating, deleting or relabeling a partition, or initializing a blank disk. It never reads the contents of your files.

**What the app stores.** Nothing. It keeps no logs, history or analytics on disk. The on-screen activity log is cleared when you close the app.

**What the app sends.** Nothing. Neon Disk Management makes no network connections. No personal data, disk information, usage statistics or diagnostics leave your device.

**Third parties.** None. The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs.

**Children.** The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

**Contact.** Use the support information on this app's Microsoft Store page.
